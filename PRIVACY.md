# WIZY RPG: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 24, 2026*

This policy covers the Android game **WIZY RPG** (package `com.holywizy.rpg`), made by the WIZY developer ("we").

## What the game collects

WIZY has **no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising ID**. We do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

The game only sends the following, to help us fix bugs:

1. **Bug reports you choose to send.** When you tap REPORT and then SEND, the game sends the text you typed together with technical details: phone model, Android version, game version, screen size, frame rate and where you are in the game (area, level, quest). Please do not type your name, e-mail or address in the report.
2. **Automatic crash reports.** If the game closed unexpectedly, the next start sends a short technical log (error messages, graphics chip, game version and what the game was doing).
3. **Play-time notes.** Short technical notes such as minutes played per area, character level and average frame rate, with a random player number made by the game. This number is not linked to your identity, your Google account or your device ID.

## How it is used and shared

This information is used **only to find and fix problems and improve the game**. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored in a private Google Form / Google Sheet that only the developer can see. We **do not sell or share** it with anyone else, and it is never used for advertising.

## Downloads

On first start the game downloads its art files from GitHub. Only standard download information (such as your IP address) is seen by GitHub, under GitHub's own privacy policy.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your phone. Bug reports contain no personal information, so they cannot be linked back to you. If you still want a report removed, send a request through the game's REPORT button or the developer contact listed on the game's Google Play page, and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
